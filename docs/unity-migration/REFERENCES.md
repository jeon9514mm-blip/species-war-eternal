## Cross-cutting requested requirements

- All 30 heroes and all 120 skills; hero/monster/boss art preserved, stable 86.4px base hero size at the reference layout, eight-direction facing and movement, readable shadows/outlines/breathing, cape/hair secondary motion.
- Ten-member formation options, projected body separation, close but legal spacing, monster separation/alignment/cohesion/avoidance and varied behaviour. No recurring teleports, collision correction spikes or pulsing body scale.
- 1024 stone PBR and micro detail, moss/bronze/patina/edge wear/cracks/AO, normal detail, wetness/footprint accents; layered rune circle, leaves/dust, glow and restrained post-processing.
- Distinct skill motifs and timings for charge/projectile/impact/afterglow, trails/afterimages/slashes, bounded particles, hit/crit/loot feedback, readable Outfit damage numbers, skill/crit/loot/monster audio and supported haptics.
- Three dedicated raid maps and distinct bosses/phases, clear ground warnings, timer, counter/stagger windows and shield/add/DPS checks, stable camera/focus, real rewards settled once.
- Simple, polished Korean UI, five original navigation tabs, original progression/equipment/summon/guardian/faction/reward systems; preserve actual menus and save protections.
- Investigate and retain the requested combat AI training/tool integration; prove useful decisions and reproducible results rather than claiming that installing a training package makes AI trained.
- Translate previous Godot-specific requests to actual Unity equivalents and verify them. Graphics labels alone are not evidence: imported GLB bytes do not mean hair/cloth rendering is implemented; Unity does not obtain Godot SDFGI by copying a number into configuration.
- Profile active worst-case hunting/raid/VFX on target hardware. Anti-aliasing and renderer features must form tested quality profiles; do not blindly stack incompatible options. Preserve smooth combat when adding stronger slow motion/hit-stop/camera effects.
- Recheck all user-supplied Meta AI effect/art links against accessible content before claiming they were incorporated. Missing/inaccessible content must be listed explicitly, not guessed.
- GitHub default-branch publication for every completed checkpoint, native Unity captures and actual build validation before declaring the full engine migration complete.
