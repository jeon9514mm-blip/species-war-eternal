# Map materials and provenance

## CC0 surface textures

Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/ — CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license

Used material sets (selected color/normal maps, 1K):
- aerial_grass_rock
- forest_ground_04
- sandstone_cracks
- cobblestone_floor_01
- mossy_rock
- rock_face_03
- bark_brown_01
- rock_boulder_cracked

Downloaded from the official `dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/` endpoints. Not every channel is used; see scene material resources.

ambientCG Ice003, https://ambientcg.com/a/Ice003 — CC0, https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Includes selected 2K color, OpenGL normal and roughness images from the official Ice003 2K JPG package.

## Original generated material art

`ornament/arena-carving.png` and `ornament/frozen-door.png` were generated in this task for individual 3D surfaces. They are not third-party game artwork and are not represented as CC0 library assets.

## Original geometry

`tools/ForestMeshAuthor.gd`, `tools/build_3d_maps.gd` and `tools/build_ice_cavern.gd` author the geometry. Final shipped maps do not require the Kenney assets evaluated during initial research.

User-supplied scene skeletons and Meta documents are archived in `docs/references/`. Their missing GLB/WebP dependencies have not been included or represented as available.
